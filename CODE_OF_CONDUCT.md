# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Critique the code, not
the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- Give and take feedback gracefully. A rejected pull request is a decision
  about scope, not a verdict on anyone.
- Be patient with people who are new to pixel art, to SVG, or to open source.
- Say what you mean plainly, and prefer a specific question to a vague
  complaint.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing
anyone's private information, or sexualised language and imagery. Nothing about
a loading indicator justifies any of it.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space the project occupies, including when someone is representing the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report anything that needs attention through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/michalstrnadel/octoloader/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer through [michalstrnadel.com](https://michalstrnadel.com).
Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not fit this
document, and may block accounts for behaviour that is repeated or severe.
